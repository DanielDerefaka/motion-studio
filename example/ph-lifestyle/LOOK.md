# The PH Lifestyle look (one example, not a default)

This is the look of the two PH Lifestyle films. It came from those films' own
inspo and brand; use it only when the person asks for this look.

The fonts are not in this repo. Mazzard Soft H and Editor's Note are
commercial faces: licence them, or swap in a free pair of the same feel
(reference/inspo.md lists free font classes).

## Type

Pairing:

- **Bold:** Mazzard Soft H Bold: captions, labels, titles, numbers.
- **Accent:** Editor's Note (Regular italic for numerals, Semibold italic for
  accent words), at 1.16em of the caption size.

Accent words are marked `*like this*` in CAPTION_CARDS. Each accent word gets
a hand-drawn brush swash under it in the film's accent colour: a filled
tapered stroke along a gentle wave, seeded per word, wiping in left to right
a few frames after the word lands. One or two accent words per card; never a
whole line.

Sizes at 1080p (16:9) / 1080×1920 (9:16):

| Element | 16:9 | 9:16 |
|---|---|---|
| Caption | 64 px, max width 1500, centre y 938 | 74 px, max width 940, centre y 1462 |
| Chapter chip numeral / title | 54 / 30 px at (64, 56) | 58 / 32 px at (56, 150) |
| Callout label title / sub | 30 / 22 px | 32 / 23 px |
| Money pop | 90 px | 100 px |
| Safe area (l, r, t, b) | 48, 1872, 48, 862 | 56, 1024, 130, 1330 |

Captions sit on a smoked pill (rgba(14,16,18,.42), radius 22) with a soft
text shadow; words rise in on a spring (30 px, slight rotate, the accent word
rotates more), and the card leaves up 22 px. Readable at phone size is the
test: hold a still at 390 px wide.

## Colour

One accent colour from the product (PH Lifestyle green #008751), white type,
smoked glass, one warning colour for spending (#e5484d). The product's own
icons keep their own colours. Don't add a palette the product doesn't have.
