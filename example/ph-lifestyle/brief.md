# PH Lifestyle: "Just Hide the HUD"

Idea: a PH Lifestyle player builds a Port Harcourt life by jumping on the
game's own HUD and phone.

## Film

- One sentence: **in PH Lifestyle you live a whole Port Harcourt life from
  your phone, in a city you share with everyone online.**
- Audience: players and would-be players on TikTok, X and Discord.
- Duration 16.8 s = 28 beats at 100 BPM (one beat = 0.6 s = 18 frames).
- 1920×1080, 30 fps, 504 frames. Separate 9:16 composition later.

## Brand block (the only part that changes per film)

- HERO: Tobi, the game's own "Senator" look (`game/media/characters/tobi.glb`,
  rigged, clips Idle / Casual_Walk / RunFast / Hip_Hop_Dance / Alert).
- APP: the live PH Lifestyle HUD as it is on www.phlifestyle.fun today
  (20 Oct 2026): the cash pill with six needs (Belle, Energy, Vibe, Water,
  Social, Fun), the place card, the online pill, the bottom bar (Home, Map,
  People, Phone, View, Hide), the action dock, the chat bar and the phone
  home screen with its real app tiles. Captured from the live DOM, not drawn.
- RESULT: the world: real footage recorded on the live site, HUD hidden.
  Every press changes what the world shows.
- SET: every HUD panel is a floating slab above the world slab, an exploded
  view of the game screen, in a dark warm studio.

## Facts that must come from the product

- Footage, UI panels, app icons, place names, prices and copy come from the
  live site. Nothing is invented: no fake player counts, no fake screens.
- The online number is whatever the live pill showed while recording.
- End card copy: the game's own line and the real address phlifestyle.fun.

## Unacceptable

- A UI panel that does not exist in the game, a made-up metric, a jump that
  changes nothing, lights blowing out the glass, the hero leaving frame.

## Deliverables

Storyboard (shotlist.md), UI texture, world contact sheet, 18 stills, the
16:9 film with sound, a poster frame, source and the render commands.
